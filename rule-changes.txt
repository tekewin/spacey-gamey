Spacey Gamey

Rule changes from the original 1984 rules (current state of the HTML game):

0) Single player for now: one ship vs a computer-generated ship.
   The human always moves first (no 2d6 roll for initiative).
   Computers and computer programs (BOBC, Crit-Hit, History, Multi-Ship,
   Accurator, Master) are not used.

1) Map is 50 x 50 hexes (original 60 x 40). Ships start on the diagonal,
   50 hexes apart. Ships can't leave the map.
   Hyperspace drops the ship on a random hex (replaces the quadrant/d10/d20 roll).

2) Rockets CAN cause critical hits, like every other weapon
   (one crit check per rocket volley).

3) Normal mode - computer ships are built with the lowest amount of credits
   in your class range. Reward: 1d4 x 1,000 credits.
   Hard mode - computer ships are built with the highest amount of credits
   in your class range. Reward: (1d4 + 2) x 1,000 credits.

4) Ships are automatically saved on your local computer in a "hangar"
   (browser storage) and can be loaded in the next battle.
   Ships can also be exported/imported as .ship.json files.

5) Ships that are destroyed remain unchanged in the hangar, so not a permanent loss.

6) After a win, the ship is automatically repaired to full hull points, all
   crit damage is fixed, and all ammo fired is restocked to its pre-battle
   level, free. Winnings can only be spent on additions - nothing can be sold.

7) Balance changes:
   Price of photons increased from 50 to 150
   Beam types V and VI get a 45 hex range to match solar weapon range
     (to-hit at 31-35: 18, 36-40: 19, 41-45: 20)
   Solar thrusters cost 1,500 (was 1,000)
   Conventional thrusters cost 1,500 (was 2,000)
   Mini-thrusters cost 1,000 (was 1,250)
   Cannons have a 10 hex range instead of 3, using the plasma to-hit numbers
   Rammers take less damage when ramming: 1/4 of the damage inflicted
     (was 1/2), and none with a de-rammer (was 1/4)
   Damage to nukes is cumulative: 10 total damage destroys a missile

8) Rulings on things the original rules left open:
   Every weapon can fire only once per turn.
   Each beam needs its own recharger and each solar weapon its own generator;
     without one it fires once per battle.
   One missile launcher fires either a nuke or a plasma, one per turn.
   Shields reduce the damage of each rocket and of plasma hits (-1 per shield).
   Nuclear missile speed: 1 hex/turn, 4 with one missile thruster, 8 with two.
     If the target is within that distance at launch, it hits that turn.
     Otherwise it flies its full speed toward the target right away, then
     again on each of the owner's turns.
   Maximum of 3 shields (conventional) or 3 sunscreen shields (solar).
