Spacey Gamey

Variations from the original rules:

0) Single player for now, one ship vs a computer generated ship

1) Ships can't leave the screen

2) Rockets cannot cause critical hits

3) Easy mode - computer ships use the lowest amount of credits in a class range 1d4 credits reward
Hard mode - computer ships use the highest amount of credits in a class range 1d4 + 2 credits reward

4) ships are automatically saved on your local computer in a "hangar" and can be loaded in the next battle.

5) ships that are destroyed remain unchanged in the hangar, so not a permanent loss.

6) balance changes: 
Price of photons increased from 50 to 150
Beam type V and VI get a 45 hex range to match solar weapons range
Solar thrusters cost 1500, conventional thrusters 1500, minithruster 1000
Cannons have a 10 hex range instead of 5. 
Rammers take less damage when ramming. 
Damage to nukes is cumulative.
